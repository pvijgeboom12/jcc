## Article 1 Who we are

JCC Congress Consultancy & Events is a full-service congress and event agency located at Sallandstraat 16-2, 1079 PP Amsterdam, the Netherlands (Chamber of Commerce no. 62440756). This statement explains how we handle personal data. You can reach us at +31 (0)6 1667 1881 or info@jcc-congress.com.

## Article 2 Our role

JCC Congress Consultancy & Events is the data controller for data we process for our own business. This includes our website, client relationships, suppliers and our own events.

For congresses, membership administration and financial administration that we carry out on behalf of an association, foundation or other client, we usually act as a data processor. The client is then the controller and decides how the data is used. This applies both when the client contracts the registration system directly and when we purchase it on the client's behalf. This is also stated in the information for the congress concerned. In that case you can send a request about your data to the client or to us; we will forward it where necessary.

## Article 3 Who this statement applies to

- Visitors to our websites and event platforms.
- Users of congress apps that we use for a congress.
- Congress participants, speakers and abstract submitters.
- Sponsors, exhibitors and their contact persons, including where no collaboration exists yet.
- Clients, suppliers and their contact persons.
- Members of associations whose membership administration we manage.

## Article 4 Personal data we process

- Contact and profile data: name, address, email address, telephone number, job title and organisation.
- Registration data: selected programme items, attendance and your BIG number or other registration number, to register accreditation points (for example in GAIA or PE-online).
- Payment and invoice data: billing address, payment status and bank details.
- Abstract and speaker data: name, affiliation, biography, photo and the content of submitted abstracts.
- App data: session choices, votes on sessions and posters, and messages to other participants.
- Correspondence: emails and other messages between you and us.
- Usage data: IP address, device data and data about your use of our website and of congress apps.
- Dietary and accessibility requirements: only if you choose to share them.
- Photos and videos: taken during events.

Dietary and accessibility requirements may reveal information about your health or religion. We process them only with your explicit consent and in principle pass them on to the caterer or venue without your name. Only where needed for your safety, for example with a severe allergy, do we also share your name. You can withdraw your consent at any time.

How we obtain data. We usually receive your data from you, for example when you register, submit an abstract or use the app. Sometimes we receive it from a client, such as a participant or membership list. We also take contact details of (potential) sponsors and exhibitors from public sources, such as company websites.

Required data. For registration and invoicing we need certain data, such as your name, email address and billing details. Without this data we cannot process your registration. The registration form shows which fields are required.

Varies by congress. The data we ask for depends on the congress. For a medical congress, for example, we ask for your BIG number for accreditation. The registration form shows which data is needed for that congress.

## Article 8 Transfers outside the European Economic Area

We process data within the EEA wherever possible. If a supplier processes data outside the EEA, we ensure appropriate safeguards. These may be a European Commission adequacy decision or the Commission's standard contractual clauses. You can request more information or a copy of these safeguards at info@jcc-congress.com.

Cloudflare and Google are US companies and may also process data in the United States. Some registration platforms also use services in the United States, for example to send emails. These transfers are covered by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

## Article 9 Cookies

Our website does not place cookies, except strictly necessary security cookies from our hosting provider Cloudflare. These are needed to protect the site against misuse. We do not use cookies or similar techniques to track you. That is why you do not see a cookie notice.

We measure visits to our website with Cloudflare Web Analytics. It works without cookies and does not track you across other websites. We only see totals, such as the number of visits per page.

If we later add third-party services, such as videos or maps, we will update this statement and ask for your consent first where required.

## Article 10 Your rights

You have the right to:

- access your personal data;
- have inaccurate data corrected;
- have your data erased;
- restrict processing;
- object to processing based on legitimate interest, and at any time to direct marketing;
- receive your data in a portable format;
- withdraw consent, without affecting processing carried out before withdrawal.

Please send your request to info@jcc-congress.com. We will respond within one month. For complex requests we may extend this by up to two months; we will let you know if so. If we are in doubt about your identity, we may ask for additional information. If we ask for a copy of your ID document, please obscure your photo, citizen service number (BSN) and document number.

These rights are not absolute. Sometimes we cannot (fully) carry out a request, for example because of a statutory retention obligation or the privacy of others. In that case we will explain why.

We do not make decisions based solely on automated processing.

## Article 11 Photos and videos at events

Photos and videos may be taken at events for reporting and promotion, for example on websites, social media or in newsletters. The material may be used both by us and by the client of the congress. If you do not want to be recognisable, please tell the registration desk or the photographer. You can also object afterwards at info@jcc-congress.com.

## Article 12 Security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data against misuse, loss and unauthorised access, including:

- encrypted connections (HTTPS) for our websites and data transfers;
- two-factor authentication on our email, hosting and website management accounts;
- access to data only for those who need it, with strong passwords;
- website and email with providers that store data securely and keep earlier versions that can be restored;
- keeping software and devices up to date.

If you have questions or suspect misuse, please contact us.

## Article 13 Changes

We may update this statement. The current version and its date are always available on our website.

## Article 14 Complaints

If you have a complaint about how we handle your data, please contact us at info@jcc-congress.com. You can also file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl).
