Facial surgery starts with looking, analysing and handling tissue with care. Anatomy, tissue layers, scarring, shape, volume and symmetry always determine the result, whether you repair a defect, correct a post-traumatic asymmetry or treat an aesthetic request.

For many OMF surgeons the step to aesthetic surgery feels big. Reconstructive surgery is a natural first step: the indication is often clear and the skills apply widely.

Open to OMF surgeons, dermatologists, ENT surgeons, plastic surgeons and others interested. Sessions are in Dutch.
